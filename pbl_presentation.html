01

Problem Statement

The Challenge

E-commerce platforms increasingly suffer from fake, biased, AI-generated, and misleading product reviews written across multiple Indian languages, often accompanied by manipulated or unrelated images. Traditional sentiment analysis systems cannot handle multilingual inputs, do not detect AI-generated content, and cannot verify the authenticity of review images.

Multilingual Gap

Reviews in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali ignored by English-only systems

AI-Generated Content

ChatGPT and Gemini used to create fake reviews at scale

Image Manipulation

Stock photos, AI-generated images, and unrelated visuals

02

Project Objectives

Multilingual Sentiment Analysis

Perform accurate sentiment analysis on reviews in multiple Indian languages using mBERT to classify as Positive, Neutral, or Negative

AI-Generated Review Detection

Detect AI-generated or fake text using RoBERTa-based detector to identify machine-generated patterns

Photo Review Verification

Verify authenticity of review images using CLIP to identify AI-generated, stock, or mismatched photos

Unified Authenticity Score

Integrate all outputs using XGBoost to generate a reliable 0-100% Authenticity Score for each review
